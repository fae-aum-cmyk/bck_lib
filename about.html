About the project

Building better access to learning at BCK.

✦
OUR PURPOSE

Knowledge should be within reach.

The BCK Digital Library is a student-led initiative created to provide easily accessible, rich study materials to students of Bishop Cipriano Kihangire Senior Secondary School Day Section. It is designed to support productive use of gadgets during holidays, free time, and independent study.

THE VISION

From gadgets to growth

01

Access

Connect students with approved notes, revision resources, past papers, and other learning materials wherever they are.

02

Productivity

Encourage students to use phones and other gadgets purposefully for learning, revision, and personal development.

03

Collaboration

Bring together the creativity of the ICT Club and the academic support of the Prefectorial Education Department.
